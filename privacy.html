Echolume privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Echolume is a puzzle game made by Scorpius Labs. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What stays on your device

Your progress (maze depths, daily streak) and your settings (sound, vibration, high-contrast walls) are saved only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not ask for an account, and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only between levels or when you choose to watch one for a reward. They never appear while you are playing a maze.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • IP address, used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (added by Google's ads SDK): to show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Vibration: for the short buzz when you bump a wall. You can turn it off in Settings.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your choices

  • Delete your game progress at any time in Settings › Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
  • For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy.

Children

Echolume is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
